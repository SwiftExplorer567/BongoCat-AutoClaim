## What This Does
============================================================
{img:01_app.png}

Bongo Cat gives you a chest every 30 minutes, but only if you click the popup. Miss it and the chest just waits there.

[b]BongoCat AutoClaim[/b] makes the game claim the chest by itself the second the timer hits 00:00. It doesn't move your mouse or press any keys, so you can keep playing something else or working.

[list]
[*]Works for both [b]Cosmetic[/b] and [b]Emote[/b] chests
[*]You still need 1,000 taps, same as normal
[*]No extra chests or drops, it only claims the one you already earned
[*]Free and open source, for Windows 10 and 11
[/list]

## Setup (1 Minute)
============================================================
[olist]
[*]Download [b]BongoCatAutoClaim.exe[/b] from [url=https://github.com/SwiftExplorer567/BongoCat-AutoClaim/releases/latest]GitHub Releases[/url].
[*]Run it. It finds your Bongo Cat folder, saves a backup and turns auto-claim on.
[*]If Bongo Cat is open, press [b]Restart game[/b]. That's it.
[/olist]

{img:02_status_timers.png}

When the top card says [b]Auto-claim is on[/b], it's working. The rings count down to your next chests.

============================================================
## Steam Updates & Undo
============================================================
{img:03_settings.png}

[b]After a game update:[/b] Steam replaces the game file and the patch is gone. Keep [i]Re-patch after Steam updates[/i] on and the app puts it back from the system tray. Nothing to do on your side.

[b]Want the original game back?[/b] Click [b]Restore original[/b]. Verifying game files in Steam does the same.

## FAQ
============================================================
[b]Windows or my antivirus shows a warning.[/b]
The exe isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may say "unknown publisher". The full source is on GitHub, and you can build it yourself.

[b]Why is the download about 90 MB?[/b]
Everything it needs is inside, so you don't have to install .NET or anything else.

[b]Does it change anything else in the game?[/b]
No. It only changes what happens when a chest becomes ready. Taps are spent exactly like a manual click.

[b]Is it safe for my account?[/b]
It only changes a local game file, but it's still a modification. Use it at your own risk.
